# References and Prior Art

Status: **Foundation v0**

Government Assistant is a separate concept and implementation. The following projects and public guidance are useful reference points for participation patterns and accountability.

Accessed: **2026-09-26**

## OECD — Guidelines for Citizen Participation Processes

https://www.oecd.org/en/publications/oecd-guidelines-for-citizen-participation-processes_f765caf6-en.html

Useful reference for the principle that citizen participation is not one mechanism. The OECD guidance describes multiple methods, including public consultations, open innovation, civic monitoring, participatory budgeting, and representative deliberative processes.

Government Assistant adopts the architectural lesson that the process should be selected according to the public question, authority, and purpose rather than reducing every issue to a generic online vote.

## Decidim

Features:

Documentation — Accountability:

https://docs.decidim.org/en/develop/admin/components/accountability.html

Documentation — Components:

https://docs.decidim.org/en/develop/features/components.html

Relevant patterns include:

- proposals;
- participatory processes;
- assemblies;
- meetings and debates;
- multiple voting/support configurations;
- participatory budgets;
- accountability/results;
- implementation progress and milestones.

Government Assistant differs by making AI-assisted problem structuring, proposer-neutral initiative handling, affected-community resolution, procedure routing, and execution accountability part of one canonical lifecycle.

## CONSUL DEMOCRACY

Features:

https://consuldemocracy.org/features/

Relevant patterns include:

- debates;
- citizen proposals;
- voting polls;
- participatory budgeting;
- collaborative legislation.

Government Assistant treats these as possible process components rather than assuming one mechanism fits every initiative.

## Design rule derived from the references

Existing participation platforms show that proposals, deliberation, voting, budgets, and accountability can be represented digitally.

Government Assistant's reference hypothesis is:

> start from the public problem, determine who is affected and who has lawful authority, expose evidence and alternatives, select an appropriate participation/decision process, and keep the initiative visible through execution and outcome verification.

These references are prior art and design inputs, not endorsements of any particular political system, party, candidate, or policy.
