# Affected Community

Status: **Foundation v0**

"Affected community" answers: **who should be made aware of, invited into, or represented in a public initiative process?**

It does **not** automatically answer who has legal authority to make a binding decision.

## Why this needs its own model

A radius around a pin on a map is sometimes useful and often insufficient.

A road proposal may affect:

- nearby residents;
- regular commuters;
- pedestrians and cyclists;
- public-transport users;
- schools and businesses;
- emergency services;
- people exposed to diverted traffic;
- a wider funding jurisdiction.

A school-capacity proposal may concern a catchment area rather than a simple circle.

A water-system proposal may follow a network topology.

## Community dimensions

A deployment may derive a draft affected community from one or more dimensions.

### Geography

- property/block;
- street segment;
- neighborhood;
- administrative district;
- municipality;
- region;
- watershed/corridor/catchment or another functional geography.

### Service relationship

- users of a transit route;
- patients of a service area;
- pupils/families in a school catchment;
- customers of a public utility;
- permit or program participants.

### Exposure

- safety risk;
- noise;
- pollution;
- congestion;
- displacement;
- construction impact;
- loss or gain of access.

### Fiscal relationship

People or institutions affected by a material budget, fee, levy, or funding allocation may be relevant stakeholders.

Fiscal relationship alone should not imply greater civic weight.

### Institutional relationship

- responsible authority;
- implementing body;
- regulator;
- public asset owner;
- emergency service;
- school/clinic/utility operator.

## Participation sets

The platform should model separate sets:

1. **Notify** — people or organizations who should receive information.
2. **Consult** — people whose evidence or views are relevant.
3. **Deliberate** — people selected or eligible for structured discussion.
4. **Support** — people eligible to endorse an agenda-setting proposal.
5. **Decide** — people or bodies legally eligible for a binding decision.
6. **Observe/audit** — actors entitled to monitor process integrity.

Do not collapse these into one "community" boolean.

## Drafting and review

Government Assistant may propose an affected-community definition, but the definition should expose:

- inputs used;
- geography/service/exposure rules;
- inclusion and exclusion criteria;
- confidence and known gaps;
- authorizing policy or procedure where applicable;
- revision history.

For consequential processes, a human or authorized rule should approve the final decision-eligibility set.

## Notification

Notifications should be event- and relevance-based.

Examples:

- "A new road-safety initiative concerns the street segment you follow."
- "The playground proposal within your verified district entered feasibility review."
- "Public consultation closes on 14 May."

The system should not use inferred political ideology or personalized persuasion profiles to decide who receives a civic notification.

## Minority and indirect effects

A majority of directly adjacent residents may not be the only affected group.

The process should surface:

- accessibility impacts;
- effects on children or people unable to participate directly;
- downstream neighborhoods;
- vulnerable or low-participation groups;
- material externalities.

This does not predetermine the decision. It prevents a narrow notification rule from making affected people invisible.

## Anti-gaming

The platform should detect and review:

- mass account creation;
- fabricated residency or service relationship;
- coordinated duplicate submissions;
- location spoofing;
- paid or automated endorsements;
- attempts to redefine a community solely to change the result.

Anti-abuse systems must themselves be auditable and provide correction paths for false positives.

## Privacy

The public interface should normally show aggregate community definitions, not expose private addresses or sensitive eligibility evidence.

Identity proof, eligibility proof, notification routing, and public profile are separate data concerns.
